Academic Levels

Our educational structure supports children and young people through each stage of their learning journey, from the early years to senior secondary school.

Nursery

Nursery 1, Nursery 2 and Nursery 3 provide a caring foundation for early learning, communication, confidence and positive routines.

Primary

Class 1, Class 2, Class 3, Class 4, Class 5 and Class 6 support growing learners as they develop foundational knowledge, study habits, responsibility and curiosity.

Secondary School

JSS 1, JSS 2 and JSS 3 guide students through the junior secondary stage, while SSS 1, SSS 2 and SSS 3 support senior secondary learning with a focus on academic growth and personal development.

Our School

Ebenezer Comprehensive Pre-Primary and Secondary School is located on Bumban Road, off Fullah Town, Kabala, Koinadugu District, Sierra Leone. We aim to provide a welcoming environment where learners are encouraged to develop knowledge, discipline, responsibility and respect.
